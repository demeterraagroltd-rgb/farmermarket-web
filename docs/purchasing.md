# Purchasing: first ERP release

Open Dashboard → Purchasing. Admins and super admins can manage suppliers, prepare
purchase orders and receive deliveries. Existing vendor records are reused as
suppliers, preserving product references.

1. Add a supplier with contacts and payment terms.
2. Create a purchase order, choosing an active **pickup location as the warehouse**.
   Add products, ordered quantities and actual supplier unit costs. No costs are
   guessed or taken from selling prices.
3. Save/edit the draft and submit it. A different super admin approves it; the
   preparer cannot approve their own order. Submitted orders can be returned with
   a reason, then edited by their preparer and resubmitted.
4. Receive against an approved order. Enter the supplier delivery reference and
   accepted/rejected quantities. Partial deliveries leave the rest outstanding.
5. Accepted quantities update available inventory in the same transaction as the
   numbered goods receipt. Rejected quantities are recorded but never become stock.
6. A super admin can close undelivered quantities with a reason. Closing does not
   remove delivered stock. Draft/submitted orders can be cancelled with a reason.

PO and GRN numbers are generated by database sequences; gaps are normal. Quantity
uses the product's existing stock unit. Carton splitting is not yet supported.
Unit changes after preparation must be resolved before receipt posting.

Receipt posting uses an operation UUID to make retries safe. Delivery references
cannot repeat within the same PO, ignoring case/outer whitespace. Posted receipts,
receipt lines and inventory history cannot be edited or deleted. Submitted and
approved PO line snapshots are protected. Stock corrections still use Inventory
with a reason referring to the GRN; a dedicated authorised receipt-reversal
workflow is a later slice.

Purchase orders, receipts and stock movements use pickup warehouses. Accepted
goods now increase that warehouse's balance and the global total together.
Warehouse reservations, transfers, opening allocations and physical counts are
described in warehouses.md. Legacy opening balances remain unassigned until staff
attribute them to their actual locations.

Supplier invoice matching, payment recording and payable balances are available
under **Invoices & payments**. See supplier-payables.md for the workflow, roles,
corrections and limits. No supplier money transfers or general-ledger entries are
posted by this release. The complete programme is in erp-plan.md.
